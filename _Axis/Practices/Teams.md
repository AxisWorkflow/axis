# Teams
> **Purpose:** Set up a feasible Team using common infrastructure and a task-specific collaboration plan.

An External may use this planning process for its own proposal Team under [Practices > ExternalTeams]. It follows the explicit owner-bound helper route; only Main accepts project changes.

Load for Team creation or a material method change. [Practices > Coordination] owns records, authority, member binding, dispatch, workspaces and acceptance. [Practices > Collaboration] owns the selected working method. Membership, logical role, provider session and actual concurrent availability are distinct.

## Setup

1. Use the supplied objective and standing instructions. Name deliverables, acceptance evidence, a separate leader and required member roles. Recommend a suitable method; ask only for missing consequential information or authority. A setup preview is not automatically another approval gate.
2. Choose or compose a pattern under [Practices > Collaboration]. State who works independently, who reviews whose artifact, whether responses and revisions are required, who synthesizes and who accepts. Main accepts project changes; the leader may coordinate the approved method without relaying every peer exchange through Main.
3. State required simultaneous participation. Check the actual execution route, live/uncertain holders and qualified storage. A three-slot route cannot silently turn an explicitly concurrent seven-member plan into waves. Report that route's limit; use an authorized suitable route or revise the plan with User.
4. Bind the actual total monetary spending and/or runtime agreement, cost basis and clock. Include synthesis, Main review and finalization. Keep call/token/message/retry forecasts separate from budgets. At a real exhausted bound preserve work and cumulative use, hold affected execution and request an extension. A question alone does not require retiring healthy members; retain contexts when feasible and continue independent work inside the grant.
5. Use `_Axis/Resources/team-plan.py {plan.json}` for an optional read-only structural preview. It checks supplied planning data, not actual access, authentication, allowance or substantive quality. Without Python, review the same fields manually. Examples under `_Axis/Resources/Team-Patterns/` are data, never authority. A custom plan is equally valid when its obligations are clear.
6. Present one short setup summary: outcome, method and deviations, leader/members, requested and observed concurrency, actual budget, exchanges/artifacts, synthesis/acceptance and material uncertainty. A complete previously approved setup proceeds without asking again.
7. Create the charter and Jobs through [Practices > Coordination]. For checked structured plans, embed `collaboration_plan` using schema `axis.team-plan/v1`; the coordinator verifies exact member slots and sufficient charter concurrency, and records its canonical SHA-256. It never authorizes dispatch from preview alone. Existing prose charters remain manually reviewed; they are not retroactively classified as checked plans.

## Participation and completion

Carry the actual workspace, peer-read access, direct-message rights, participation period, method, artifact references and result requirements in each member's prompt. Every member can communicate directly with authorized peers, write its own workspace and review published peer artifacts. Main integrates shared project writes. Give a departing or replaced member a verified handoff; an administrative close does not prove its process stopped.

Completion requires the actual deliverables and required reviews, not a count of Messages. Preserve dissent, partial results, unavailable observations and Main interventions. Distinguish a worker return, a complete study, an accepted product change and a released implementation. A failed attempt can support a later successful continuation without rewriting the failure.

## Future multiple users

Keep human principal, delegation, agent, logical role and running session separate. Ownership and visibility must be explicit. A role name, OS account, folder access or hash is not authentication. Local cooperating-writer tests do not establish cross-machine access control, distributed locking or private human discussions. Those capabilities require their own qualification; current single-writer integration remains.
