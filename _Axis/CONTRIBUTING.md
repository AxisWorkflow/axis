# Contributing to Axis Workflow
> **Purpose:** Require a copyright assignment before accepting an Axis contribution.

Thank you for considering a contribution.

Axis accepts a contribution only after its contributor has executed the
[Contributor License Agreement and Copyright Assignment](/_Axis/CLA.md). The
agreement assigns copyright in the contribution to the Axis copyright owner.
A pull-request checkbox, `Signed-off-by` line, issue comment, or submission by
itself does not replace an executed agreement unless the project has deployed
an electronic acceptance process that records the exact agreement version,
contributor identity, authority, timestamp, and affirmative acceptance.

Before submitting:

1. Read the CLA and obtain independent advice if needed.
2. If an employer or another entity may own the work, obtain its written
   authorization or have that entity execute the agreement.
3. Identify every third-party component and its license in the submission.
4. Do not submit secrets, confidential information, or material You cannot assign.
5. Contact https://simaxis.ai/ for the current signature process.

The project may decline or close a contribution for which a valid agreement is
not on file. Acceptance of an agreement does not guarantee that a contribution
will be merged.
